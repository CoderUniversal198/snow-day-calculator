# Snow Day Calculator

Snow Day Calculator is an application that calculates the probability of school closing because of snowy weather.

## 🌐 Check the website: https://www.snowdaycalculation.com/

## Features

- Calculates the probability of a snow day.
- Shows live weather conditions.
- Provides weather-dependent estimations of school closures.
- Type in your city or ZIP code to receive local weather condition information.
- Get information about snow, temperature, and wind conditions.

## How It Works

The Snow Day Calculator takes into account weather data and applies the logic of forecasting to calculate the probability of school closures.

1. Input your city or ZIP code.
2. Obtain local weather information.
3. Compute the estimated probability of snow days.
4. Get your forecast.

## Use It Online

Go to [Snow Day Calculator](https://www.snowdaycalculation.com/) to get your snow day forecast for free.
